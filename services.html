Where I work

Any birth setting, no agenda

Hospital

I’m a former operating room nurse, so hospitals are familiar ground. I know the language, the rhythms, and how to help you be heard in a busy room — including if your birth becomes a cesarean.

Birth center

I’ve given birth in birth centers myself and supported many families in them. If you’re trying to find one that fits — or aren’t sure one exists near you — I can help you look.

Home

I’ve had two of my own babies at home, the most recent in July 2026, and I spent 2024 and 2025 assisting midwives at home births. I know how a home birth actually runs and how to be useful in someone’s own space.
