Let’s book a free chat

Fifteen minutes on video, no cost and no obligation. You ask me anything you like and get a feel for whether I’m the right person to have with you.

Most families talk to more than one doula before deciding, and I’d encourage that. This matters too much to settle on a person you only half clicked with.

What happens next

Fill in the form and I’ll get back to you to find a time that works. If you know your due date and where you’re planning to give birth, telling me now saves us both a step — my availability depends on when you’re due.

If you’re in labor right now

This form isn’t monitored around the clock. Please call your midwife, your OB, or your hospital — and if this is an emergency, call 911.

Before this goes live

The form needs a real endpoint. Two easy options: create a free Formspree form and paste the ID into the action above, or deploy to Netlify and add data-netlify="true" to the form tag instead — Netlify then emails submissions with no third party.

Also needed: Catherine’s business email address and the real Facebook page URL (both are placeholders in the footer right now).
